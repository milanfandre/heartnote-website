What we collect, and why

Last updated August 5, 2026

This policy explains what Heart Note collects when you use this website or order a song, why we collect it, who else sees it, and how to ask us to delete it. We have written it in plain English rather than legal language.

What we collect

What you type into the order form

To write your song we need to know about the person it is for. That means:

  • The occasion, and who the song is for
  • Your name, so they know who it is from
  • The story you write about them, anything you ask us to include, and any other notes
  • Your preferred style of music, and a voice if you choose one

The story you write is the most personal thing we hold. It often contains names, dates, and details about people other than you. We use it only to write and produce your song, and to answer you if you contact us about your order.

Payment and contact details

Payment is handled entirely by Stripe. You enter your card details on Stripe's own checkout page, not ours. We never see or store your card number. Stripe passes us your email address and name so we can send your song and your receipt.

Information collected automatically

When you browse the site we record which pages you view, where you arrived from, roughly which advertisement or campaign brought you, whether you are on a phone or a computer, and a random identifier for your visit. We use this to understand which pages work and which do not.

About the scrambled email

When you buy, we tell Meta that a purchase happened so we know which advertisements are worth paying for. Your email is turned into an irreversible string of characters before it is sent, so Meta cannot read your address, but it can match it against an account it already holds. If you would rather we did not do this at all, email us and we will exclude you.

How long we keep it

  • Your order and your song. Kept while your private song page is live, so you can return to it. Ask us and we will delete both.
  • Payment records. Kept by Stripe, and by us in summary, for as long as tax and accounting rules require.
  • Website analytics. Kept in a form that is not tied to your name.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete it
  • Send you a copy
  • Stop using it for advertising measurement

Email paul@heartnote.music and we will respond within 30 days. We will not charge you or treat you differently for asking.

If you are in the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in California, we confirm that we have not sold or shared personal information for money in the past twelve months.

Children

Heart Note is meant for adults buying gifts. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will remove it.

Where your information is held

Our suppliers store data in the United States. If you order from outside the United States, your information will be transferred there, protected by the standard contractual terms those companies operate under.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will be mentioned in an email to recent customers.
