Our story

There is a person behind
every Heart Note.

Heart Note is Paul and his team. Not a template, not a form that spits out a jingle. When you send us your story, a person reads it and decides what the song should sound like.

Paul has been making records for a long time

He trained at NYU Tisch School of the Arts, then spent years behind studio desks and in front of crowds, producing for other people and learning what makes a song land.

Heart Note came out of a simple observation. The songs that moved people most were never the biggest productions. They were the ones written about someone specific, with details only that person would recognise. So that became the whole job.

Paul and his team write and produce every Heart Note, and nothing reaches you until it sounds like something worth keeping.
